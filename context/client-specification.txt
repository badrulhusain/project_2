PRODUCT
EventSense is a campus event platform that fixes miscalculation of food, seating, parking and electricity. Attendees browse events, book a seat, pre-order food, pick a parking zone, get a QR ticket and timed notifications. Admins create events and use a Smart Planner that turns bookings into an attendance forecast with food, seat, parking and power recommendations, run QR check-in and food collection on the day, and read a planned-vs-actual report with a savings dashboard.
